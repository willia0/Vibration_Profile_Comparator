32	PG 17 — Dynamic Loading
32.1	PG 17 — General
Purpose:
Influence of Sine, Noise and Shock Profiles on Connectors.
Prerequisite:
The resonance behavior of the clamping device must be checked before the test. Deviations of > + 6 dB in the frequency range to be tested are permissible if an influence on the result is not to be expected. The verification can be carried out by suitable calculation tools (FEM) and/or by resonance investigations on a vibration system. In both examination methods, the test sample mass must also be taken into account. Parameters: 5 Hz – 2 000 Hz – 5 Hz, 10 m/s2, 1 octave/min, 1 pass.
Contact Validation
Lot size:
Contact parts:
At least 10 contact parts per variant, maximum nominal conductor cross-section, unless otherwise specified.
Test samples from different cavities (e.g. double stroke) are not explicitly considered.
Housing:
Sealed or unsealed, depending on the area of application.
Enclosure validation
Batch size
At least 3 enclosures
Fully equipped, at least 10 electrically monitored channels (can contain several contacts in series), divided into edge areas and center, as well as locking near and far
Test samples from different cavities (mold nests) are not considered.
Nominal conductor cross-sections: 
To be determined on a case-by-case basis, preferably all nominal conductor cross-sections are divided among the samples.
Line insulation:
The insulation material must withstand at least the limit temperature TGrenz of the test object (corresponds to the continuous service temperature according to the data sheet).
Additional information on E 0.2:
	E 0.2 (contact validation):
The contact resistance is initially measured before the first axis and after each room axis in the standard climate (test samples must be tempered through) in the clamped and hardwired state. The test samples are then converted to the next axis.
	E 0.2 (Housing Validation):
The contact resistance is initially measured before the first axis and after each room axis in the standard climate (test samples must be tempered through) in the clamped and hardwired state. The test samples are then converted to the next axis.
Series connection of the contacts is permitted during housing validation. However, the total resistance may only increase as far as it is Table 6 for a contact. If this criterion is not met, the individual resistances of the contacts must be measured.

32.3	PG 17 — Clamping on the oscillating table
The control sensors must be placed in such a way that the test samples are not exposed to unacceptably high or too low loads.
The test setup must be adjusted in such a way that there is no vibration exaggeration by the unit connection, unless this is the subject of the test.
If necessary, the accelerometers for the control signal can be attached to the genset connection. However, it must be ensured that the sensor does not influence the result.
The fixation points must be designed in such a way that the continued line has no influence on the test pattern.
Unless otherwise specified, the cable fixations oscillate in phase.
Enclosure validation:
In the case of "flying" connector systems, the original fasteners (e.g. baying elements) must be reproduced or used.
The original attachment points, e.g. screw-on eyes, are to be used.
Contact validation:
In the case of contact validation, the housings must be fixed on the vibration holder in such a way that there is no influence on the contact system, see Fig. 28 and Fig. 29.

B 17.0	Resonance frequency of contacting
	The purpose of this test is to determine where the resonance frequencies of the test samples are in the three test levels X, Y and Z. The test takes place at Normklima.
In this case, the resonance frequency of the housing parts, including contacts and cables, under sinusoidal vibration must be provided.
Vibration transducers of the smallest dimensions must be applied to the housings that are not firmly screwed to the oscillating table. Alternatively, non-contact measurement, e.g. by means of a laser vibrometer, is permitted.
Proof must be provided on a test sample.

32.6	PG 17 — Requirements
The maximum values of the resistors from Table 6 are not exceeded in the pre-, intermediate and post-measurement with standard climate.
No functionally relevant damage must occur (housing including locking and contact parts);
Evaluation of the contact surfaces as defined in this document (see section13);
No power interruptions shall occur during the test at load profiles V1 to V6 and during shock loading. A current interruption is present when the resistance exceeds the value of 7 Ω longer than 1 μs.
